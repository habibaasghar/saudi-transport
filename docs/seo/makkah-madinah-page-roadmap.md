## 3. Priority 3 — vehicle × city pages

| Item | Decision | Why |
|---|---|---|
| GMC private transfer Makkah / Madinah | **Section on each hub, not a page** | Phase 1 found that GMC demand is attached to routes ("makkah to madinah gmc"), and that route titles already carry "GMC or Staria". GMC Yukon seat and luggage figures are **not confirmed**, so a GMC page could not say what a buyer needs to know. Six city × vehicle pages would be near-duplicates (SEO-RULES §2). |
| SUV private transfer Makkah / Madinah | Section on each hub | As above; SUV models (Tahoe, Suburban, Prado) are confirmed but have no capacity figures. |
| Sedan private transfer Makkah / Madinah | Section on each hub | As above; sedans attract lower-value leads (owner feedback 2026-11-14), so they are not given their own landing pages. |
| National vehicle pages | Already exist: /fleet/ and the luxury SUV pages | Revisit a `/fleet/gmc-yukon/` page once the owner confirms GMC Yukon seats, luggage and a real photo. |
