# RideSaudi — New Image Requirements (Acquisition Sheet)

Date: 2026-11-23. Each row is **one asset**. The "Used on" column lists every page it serves, so one photo often fixes several pages. Filenames are final: drop the file into `public/images/` with exactly this name and it can be wired in. There are 70 page assets plus 7 fleet photos. Start with Critical and High.

## Sourcing rules (non-negotiable)

- Preferred sources, in order:
  1. The owner's own photos of real partner vehicles, drivers and jobs, taken with consent, plates blurred.
  2. Licensed stock (Shutterstock, Getty, Adobe Stock, iStock) on a standard or extended licence.
  3. Official manufacturer media, only where the press terms allow commercial use.
  4. AI generation **only** for generic scenes (an interior detail, a road at dusk). **Never** use AI for a named real place (an airport, a mosque, a terminal) or to show a "RideSaudi" vehicle or driver.
- No competitor, dealer, hotel, airline or government logos. No faces without a model release. No licence plates. No watermarks.
- Vehicles: only owner-confirmed models (Camry, Sonata, Taurus, Yukon, Suburban, Tahoe, Prado, Staria, luxury/VIP SUV). Mercedes S/V-Class and Yukon XL/Denali are **not** confirmed fleet: ask the owner before buying photos of them.
- Sister-site photos (taxisaudiarabia.com) are allowed only after re-crop, rename, re-export and metadata strip (existing rule).
- Masters: ≥1920×1080 (16:9), subject centre or centre-right, quiet area on the left third. Card crops (3:2) and share images (1200×630) are produced automatically.

## AI prompt template (generic scenes only)

> Photorealistic editorial photograph, [SUBJECT], natural [blue-hour / golden-hour / soft morning] light, shot on a 35mm full-frame camera at f/2.8, shallow depth of field, muted navy and sand colour palette, clean uncluttered composition with negative space on the left third, no text, no logos, no licence plates, no visible faces, 16:9, high detail, not stylised, not CGI.

Example for "Rear cabin of a luxury SUV": replace [SUBJECT] with *rear-seat cabin of a modern luxury SUV with black leather seats and soft ambient lighting, a bottle of water in the door pocket, evening city lights blurred through the window*.
