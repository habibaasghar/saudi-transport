## Rules for GSC decisions
- Under 30 impressions: wait.
- Wrong URL ranking: strengthen links and answer on the right page before any redirect.
- Clicks and no enquiry: check mobile recording of the form, WhatsApp text and answer speed.
- Arabic query without a page: needs suggestions or GSC evidence **and** confirmed service.

## Fastest real leads, outside SEO
Organic will be slow on a new domain. Faster: small Google Ads tests on لوبد + city and car transport (your decision and budget), partner referrals, and direct outreach to rental yards and contractors.
