## What seems to make customers contact them
1. Number visible and tappable first. Most have a call link before the main heading.
2. A price signal: a table, "from", or "fixed before we move".
3. WhatsApp that asks the right questions (satha-plus, namajed).
4. Photos of trucks at work.
5. City + service in the first line.

## How leads are qualified
Car: does it run, from, to, when. Lowbed: machine, weight, route. B2B: company, quantity, period. Few ask all of it; most rely on a call.
