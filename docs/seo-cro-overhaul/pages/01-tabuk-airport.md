### Who books this (sections)

**For companies: project teams and site visitors**
Tabuk is the main airport for people working on projects across the north-west. Engineers, auditors, consultants and contractor crews fly in, then need a reliable car to a hotel or a site reception the same day. Your travel coordinator can send one enquiry for a whole rotation (names, flights, sites) and receive one quotation. A [corporate transport arrangement for NEOM and Tabuk projects](services/corporate-transportation/neom-tabuk) *(link added when page 4 is live)* covers repeat airport runs.

**For families and visitors**
Families visiting relatives in Tabuk, travellers starting a road trip to [AlUla with a private driver](services/tourism-transport/alula), and people heading on to Jordan book the same way. The Hyundai Staria takes 7 passengers and 10 or more bags ✅ (owner-confirmed), which suits families arriving with luggage for a long stay.

### FAQs (6)
1. **Which airport is "Tabuk Airport"?**
   Prince Sultan bin Abdulaziz Airport, code TUU, which serves Tabuk city. If your ticket says NUM, you are landing at NEOM Bay Airport on the coast instead; see [NEOM Bay Airport transfers](services/airport-transfers/neom-bay-airport) *(page 2)*.
2. **Can you meet a late-night flight at Tabuk Airport?**
   Send the flight details. Night pickups depend on partner availability for that date, and the quotation confirms it. WhatsApp replies run 8am–12am KSA, so send late-night bookings in advance.
3. **Can the driver take our team straight to a NEOM site?**
   The driver can take you to the gate or reception point your employer names. Entry to project sites needs your company's pass. RideSaudi cannot arrange site access.
4. **How long is the drive from Tabuk Airport to AlUla?**
   Roughly [VERIFY: 3.5–4 hours] by road. Many visitors fly into AlUla (ULH) instead; compare with [AlUla Airport transfers](services/airport-transfers/alula-airport).
5. **Can my company get one quotation for several arrivals?**
   Yes. List each traveller's flight, date and destination in one enquiry and you receive a combined quotation.
6. **Can I book a car from Tabuk Airport to Jordan?**
   You can request it. Whether a Saudi-plated car can cross, or a handover at the border is needed, is confirmed per trip [VERIFY WITH VENDOR]. Passports and visas are the traveller's responsibility.
